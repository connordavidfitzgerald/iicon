# Editing the IICON website

Everything on the site is edited in one place: **iicon.ca/studio**

Sign in with the email address you were invited on. Nothing you type goes live
until you press **Publish**, so it's safe to look around.

## What's where

The list down the left side follows the site itself:

|                   |                                                        |
| :---------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home Page**     | Everything on the front page, top to bottom            |
| **About Page**    | The intro statement and the two values columns         |
| **Work Page**     | The heading and which projects appear in the grid      |
| **Contact Page**  | Just how the page appears in Google and when shared    |
| **Case Studies**  | Your individual project pages, and the discipline tags |
| **Services**      | The four services listed on the home page              |
| **Testimonials**  | Client quotes                                          |
| **Site Settings** | Menu, email, phone, social links, footer, default SEO  |

The bigger pages are split into tabs across the top — Home Page has Hero, Our
Approach, Awards, Testimonials, Photo Strip, Services, Projects and SEO. Each
field has a short note under its name explaining where it shows up.

## Adding a case study

1. **Case Studies → All Case Studies → the + button.**
2. Fill in the **Project** tab:
    - **Project name** — the big heading, e.g. "Mobile Tire".
    - **Web address** — press **Generate** to build it from the name. This becomes
      the page's URL. Once a project is live, changing this breaks any links
      people have already shared.
    - **Disciplines** — the grey line under the name. Pick from the list, or
      create a new one right there if you need a tag that doesn't exist yet.
    - **Project description** — the text in the grey panel. Just type; each
      paragraph is spaced for you.
    - **Awards** — one line per award. If there's more than one, put "Winner of
      the:" in the _Awards intro line_ above.
3. Fill in the **Images** tab:
    - **Thumbnail** — the square image for the project grids. It's shown in black
      and white until someone hovers it, so pick something with strong contrast.
    - **Project images** — the tall column of images on the project page, in the
      order you arrange them. Drag to rearrange.
    - Every image asks for a **description**. Write what's in the picture — it's
      what screen readers read out, and Google uses it too.
4. **SEO & Sharing** is optional. Leave it blank and the project name is used.
5. Press **Publish**.

A new case study has its own page immediately, but it won't appear in any grid
until you add it. To list it, open **Work Page** and add it to **Projects**; to
put it on the front page too, open **Home Page → Projects** and add it to
**Featured projects**. Both lists are drag-to-reorder, and the home grid looks
best with an even number.

## Changing images

Click an image, then **Replace**. After uploading you can drag the little circle
on the image to set the focal point — that's the part that stays visible when
the image is cropped to a different shape.

## Seeing your changes before they're live

Open the **Presentation** tab at the top of the Studio. The site appears beside
your editing panel, and any text you can edit gets a pencil icon when you hover
it — click it to jump straight to that field. Changes show up as you type. They
still aren't public until you press Publish.

## A few things worth knowing

- **Contact details live in one place.** Your email and phone number appear in
  the footer and on the About page, but you only edit them once, under **Site
  Settings → Contact & Social**.
- **Nothing is ever really deleted.** Every document keeps a full history —
  open the **⋯** menu and choose _Review changes_ to see past versions or restore
  one.
- **The site takes a minute to update.** After you press Publish the site
  rebuilds itself; give it a minute or two before checking iicon.ca.
- **Red dots mean a required field is empty.** The document won't publish until
  they're filled in. Hover the dot to see what's missing.
